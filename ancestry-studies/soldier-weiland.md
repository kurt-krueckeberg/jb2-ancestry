# Identity of Soldat Weiland

[Hans Hermann Weiland](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/PXMY-D76) who
was a grenadier at the Bückeburg palace according to his
[marriage record](xref:genealogy/petzen/pet-m-1730a) is apparently the **Soldat 
Weiland** whose wife was the baptism sponsor of [Dorothea Elisabeth Krückeberg.](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/P35S-CJG),
on 29 January 1736, where she was called **Soldat Weilands Frau** ("Soldier Weiland\s wife).

Hans Hermann Weiland would then be Johann Heinrich Krückeberg's brother-in-law because he married
[Ilse Maria Vahlsing](https://www.familysearch.org/en/tree/person/details/G8S2-LZ1), who
was the sister of Johann Hrinrich  Krückeberg's wife, [Anna Elisabeth Vahlsing](xref:genealogy/petzen/pet-b-1736a).

Hans Hermann was born in Berenbusch, according to his [baptism record](xref:genealogy/petzen/pet-b-1705a), on 24 May 1705.
Hans Hermann Weiland's [marriage record](xref:genealogy/petzen/pet-m-1730a) says he is from
Berenbusch. But if he is the same **Weiland Soldat von Evesen**("Weiland soldier from Evesen") mentioned in
the [baptism of son Hans Heinrich](xref:genealogy/petzen/pet-b-1736b), he had by then moved to
Evesen.
